### Core Design Principles & Anti-Slop Guidelines
1. **Brief Inference**: Infer the user's intent, target audience, page type, and visual vibe before building UI.
2. **The Three Dials**:
   - `DESIGN_VARIANCE`: Controls visual asymmetry and layout variety.
   - `MOTION_INTENSITY`: Controls animation physics and transition depth.
   - `VISUAL_DENSITY`: Controls padding, whitespace, and typographic contrast.
3. **Typography & Hierarchy**: Pair distinctive display fonts with refined body fonts. Maintain clear typographic scales and WCAG AA contrast.
4. **Layout & Space**: Avoid generic 3-column grids or AI-purple gradients. Use deliberate spatial rhythm, mathematical border radii, and clean high-contrast neutral foundations.
5. **Interactive Feedback**: Every interactive element must provide immediate, high-quality visual and touch feedback without broken click stubs or missing state updates.
